## Direction contract

THESIS: The familiar portfolio interface taken deliberately and executed to a craft level the category rarely reaches. It refuses the template's ornamental theming — glyph strips, numbered cards, uniform icon-card grids, shadow soup — and replaces it with detail that encodes real information. Every technical flourish is true or it does not ship.

OWN-WORLD: Cool paper ground with a blue-grey cast, never cream, never pure white; cool near-black ink; neutrals plus one accent reserved for actions and live values. A fine engineering grid sits behind at 3–5% contrast, major line every tenth. Cards carry hairline borders with drafting corner ticks instead of drop shadows. Archivo for display and body, Archivo Expanded for the nameplate; Azeret Mono strictly for figures, units, and measurement, never as a label costume. Tabular numerals, correct unit spacing (5 kN, 2nd / 12). Selection, caret, scrollbar, focus ring, and underline offset themed from the palette.

STORY: The visitor learns who Sam is in one line, believes the Polaris work because each claim carries a number they can check, and takes any of three equal actions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky hairline nav across the top. Name at nameplate scale, left, with the positioning line beneath it at reading scale. Three equal actions in a row directly under. The Sailbot entry begins above the fold with its first real figure already visible. No hero stat row, no eyebrow, no required photograph.

FORM: The category standard, the standing exit, chosen by the user after two safer-register re-rolls; kind canon; seed key 8740da11.
